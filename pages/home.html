Do I have to be on camera?

Not at all. Some clients love being on camera, some never want to be. I plan your content around whatever you're comfortable with, from behind-the-scenes and product reels to fully talking-head videos.

How much of my time does this take?

About one film day a month. I write the scripts before we film, so you just show up. After that, you approve finished videos from your phone.

What's the Rella membership?

Rella is where your content calendar lives. For $45 a month you can see what's coming up and approve videos from your phone, and you get a text when something new is ready, so nothing sits waiting.

Am I locked into a long contract?

No. Plans are month to month and billed at the start of each month. You can cancel with 30 days' written notice.

Is ad spend included?

No. Plans cover creating (and, with full management, posting) your content. Ad spend is separate, and ads management can be added once your page has a strong identity.

What if I film something myself?

Send it over. I'll edit it into a finished reel for $50, on top of your plan.
